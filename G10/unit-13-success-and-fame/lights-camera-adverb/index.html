HOW TO PLAY adverb charades
1

THE STAR

Reads the secret card and acts it out — the action, done in that manner.

2

THE TEAM

Shout out the action, then work out the adverb together.

3

WRITE IT

One teammate writes the adverb on the board — no rubbing out, no correcting!

THE STAR MUST NOT…

  • ✕talk or make word sounds
  • ✕mouth the words
  • ✕point at things in the room
  • ✕write letters in the air

THE STAR CAN…

  • ✓use face, body and hands
  • ✓show what first, then how
  • ✓nod or shake their head

THE -LY SPELLING RULES
1
Ends in -l or -e
→ add -ly
special → speciallynice → nicely
!
Exceptions — learn these!
true → trulywhole → whollyfull → fully
2
Ends in -y
→ remove y, add -ily
happy → happily
3
Consonant + le
→ remove e, add -y
horrible → horriblyidle → idly
4
Ends in -ic
→ add -ally
enthusiastic → enthusiastically

-LY… BUT NOT AN ADVERB!
