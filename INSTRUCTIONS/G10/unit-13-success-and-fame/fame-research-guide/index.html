How to Research Your Celebrity

Read this first. Follow the steps in order.
Wikipedia and AI are maps, not destinations.
  • Never list them as one of your 3 sources
  • Click through to the real article and use that
Step 1 10 minutes

Get the big picture

  • Read the Wikipedia page or ask AI for a short overview
  • Find the basics: when famous, why, the 3–4 key events
  • No notes yet — just get to know your person
Step 2 Using Wikipedia

Find real sources

  • Scroll to the bottom → References
  • Each small number [1] links to a real source
  • Click the ones about your 5 questions
  • Open and read the original article
Step 2 Using AI

Find real sources

  • Ask the AI to suggest sources
  • Click every link — check it is real
  • AI sometimes invents articles and links
  • Link won't open, or says something different? Don't use it.

Check that each source is good
