Step 1 — Research (homework)

Fill in your note sheet by hand:

  • How did they become famous in this era?
  • Who controlled their public image?
  • How did fans find out about / interact with them?
  • What did fame cost them (privacy, safety, mental health, control)?
  • What did they do with their fame — charity/awareness, stayed neutral, or abused it?

Step 1 — Rules

  • Write by hand. Keywords and short phrases only — no full sentences.
  • Max 5 words per bullet.
  • Write only inside the boxes — no extra pages, no back of the sheet.
  • Use at least 3 good sources: website + article title.

Step 2 — Essay (in class, no devices)

Use only your note sheet. 4 paragraphs · about 350–450 words · on paper.

Paragraph 1

Who is the person, what era, and how did they become famous?

Paragraph 2

Who controlled their image? How did fans find out about them?

Paragraph 3

What did fame cost them? What did they do with their fame?

Paragraph 4

Do they deserve their fame? Why or why not?

Step 2 — Essay

Use at least 2 contrast words:

althoughhoweverdespitenevertheless…

Hand in your essay and your note sheet at the end of class.
